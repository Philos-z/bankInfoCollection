## Remaining duplication on the official bonus page

The official Amex article repeats some offers in prose and later card/comparison sections. GPT-5.6 Sol still
emitted repeated extraction records for several offers, including Blue Card, PAYBACK and BMW Card examples.
This is treated primarily as an entity-resolution problem rather than requiring the LLM to be perfectly
deduplicated.

The validator now protects this with offline regression cases:

- normalized exact aliases such as `Blue Card` vs `American Express Blue Card` can rule-match;
- `BMW Premium Card Carbon` vs `BMW Card Carbon` can be sent to the conservative AI arbiter;
- `Gold Card` vs `Gold Card Rosé` cannot bypass AI arbitration;
- `card_name = null` with otherwise strong matching evidence is sent to AI arbitration instead of immediately
  creating a new campaign.

## Interpretation

The original `card_name = null` concern was not reproduced with GPT-5.6 Sol after the prompt change: all 25
second-run extractions had a card name. The remaining Amex duplicate risk is mainly repeated/variant naming on
the same source page, now covered by the entity-resolution regressions.
